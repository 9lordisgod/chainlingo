# Chainlingo product (scaffold)

## Audience

People learning crypto / Solana fundamentals on a Seeker phone, Duolingo-style: short daily lessons, streaks, XP.

## In scope (MVP)

- Daily lesson cards + practice questions (content from private pack)
- Streaks, XP, level gates
- Optional MWA wallet connect for progress sync identity
- Free track + paid advanced levels / subscription flag in schema

## Out of scope

- Trading, DEX, sniping, custody, seed phrases in app UI
- Autonomous on-chain actions
- Embedding full answer keys in the public repo

## Path inspiration

Six high-level learning arcs (names only; content private):

1. Philosophy & cypherpunk basics
2. Money & Bitcoin literacy
3. Privacy & OpSec
4. Solana fundamentals
5. Mobile / Seeker safety
6. On-chain reading (explorer literacy) — no trading

Exact lesson text and questions live in `chainlingo-content`.
